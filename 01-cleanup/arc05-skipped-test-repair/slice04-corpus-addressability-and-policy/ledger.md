## What Worked

- Reusing the Slice03 stable case IDs kept selection policy simple: plain text
  manifests, no new dependencies, and deterministic selection.
- Keeping manifest/named selection before filter/limit preserved local
  iteration controls without letting substring filters become policy.
- Making JSON assertion behavior explicit lets the next slice wire tests or CI
  without changing the measurement substrate again.
